Four layers.
One fairly obvious point.

The entire communications philosophy, disassembled in glorious, completely unnecessary detail.

PRINCIPLE 01 / 04

Make it simple.

I didn't need a breakdown of the bread's operating model. Members shouldn't need a glossary to understand their next step.

Find the balance.

Too much information and the point gets buried. Too little and you've lost the substance. A surprisingly delicate cheese-to-content ratio.

Timing is everything.

A toastie at lunch is perfect. A retirement message after someone has already made their decision? Considerably less useful.

Make action obvious.

Mine was “careful, the filling's hot”. Theirs might be “check your beneficiaries”. Say what people actually need to do.
